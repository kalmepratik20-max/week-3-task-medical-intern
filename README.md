# Week 3 Task – Literature Review and Critique

## Overview
This repository contains my Week 3 internship submission: a mini literature
review and critique titled **"SGLT2 Inhibitors in Heart Failure."**

The task was to review recent published studies on a medical treatment,
critically analyze their methods and findings, and discuss relevance to
clinical practice.

## Contents
- `Week3_Literature_Review.docx`: the full literature review and critique

## Document Structure
- Introduction (focus area and justification)
- Review of five selected studies (four randomized trials and one meta-analysis)
- Critical analysis (strengths, weaknesses, potential biases, comparison)
- Implications for clinical practice
- Conclusion
- References
- Explanation of thought process

## Approach
- Searched PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov for relevant studies
- Summarized each study's methodology, key results and conclusions
- Critically evaluated study design, bias and generalizability
- Compared findings across studies and linked them to clinical practice
